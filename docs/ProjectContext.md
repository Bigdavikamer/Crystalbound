# Crystalbound - Project Context

## Overview

Crystalbound is a Roblox adventure RPG focused on exploration, mining, RNG-based discoveries, crafting, collection, and long-term progression.

The player explores mysterious worlds, mines valuable resources, discovers rare crystals, crafts equipment, completes missions, and builds their personal collection.

The goal is to create a satisfying progression loop where every action has the possibility of discovery.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

The main player loop:

1. Explore worlds
2. Mine resources
3. Discover rare materials and crystals
4. Upgrade equipment
5. Unlock new areas
6. Complete missions
7. Expand collection
8. Repeat with higher progression

---

# Main Game Systems

## Mining System

Mining is one of the primary gameplay mechanics.

Players interact with resource nodes to obtain:

- Common materials
- Rare materials
- Crystals
- Special discoveries

Mining progression should feel rewarding and encourage exploration.

---

## RNG System

RNG is a major part of Crystalbound.

Players can discover:

- Rare crystals
- Unique materials
- Special equipment
- Exclusive cosmetics

RNG should always feel exciting while maintaining fair progression.

Luck should improve through gameplay, upgrades, missions, and achievements.

---

## Equipment System

Equipment is divided into multiple categories.

Main categories:

- Pickaxes
- Bracelets
- Hoods
- Hats
- Mantles

Pickaxes are primarily RNG-based.

Other equipment can be crafted, earned through missions, or obtained through special progression paths.

---

## F2P Philosophy

Crystalbound should be enjoyable for free players.

Free players should have access to:

- Craftable equipment
- Meaningful upgrades
- Long-term goals
- Collection progression

Premium features should enhance convenience or cosmetics, not completely block progression.

---

# Progression Philosophy

The game should reward:

- Time invested
- Exploration
- Smart decisions
- Collection completion

Players should always have another goal to pursue.

---

# Inspiration

Crystalbound takes inspiration from:

- RNG collection games
- Mining/progression games
- Adventure exploration games

However, Crystalbound aims to create its own identity through its world, systems, and progression design.
