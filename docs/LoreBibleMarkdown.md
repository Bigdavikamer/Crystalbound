# Crystalbound Lore Bible — Era I

## Premise

Magic is not a tool—it is a living force woven into the world itself.

Long ago, the world existed in harmony with living crystals. People did not command magic; they formed relationships with it.

Then came **The Fracture**.

No one knows what caused it.

The sky split.
Civilizations vanished.
The land became scarred.
Magic changed forever.

Thousands of years later, settlements survive only within protected regions while vast danger zones reclaim the old world.

The player is not a chosen hero.

They are simply someone whose curiosity awakens an ancient crystal in a way nobody has witnessed for centuries.

That single event begins a journey that uncovers the forgotten history of the world.

---

## The Central Mystery

What truly caused The Fracture?

Every expansion should answer one question while introducing two more.

The truth should unfold over years of updates rather than a single storyline.

---

## World Design Philosophy

Each world represents another scar left by The Fracture.

Danger zones are not simply combat arenas—they are places where reality never fully healed.

Every environment should tell part of history through architecture, creatures, weather, music, and exploration rather than exposition.

---

## Player Experience

Players should constantly think:

- "I wonder what happened here."
- "What is this crystal trying to tell me?"
- "Why is this place different?"
- "I should come back later."

Curiosity—not grinding—is the driving force of progression.
