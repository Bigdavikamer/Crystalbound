Crystalbound Lore Bible

(Version 1 — The First Era)

This should be your first world only.

The rest of the world expands from here.

THE WORLD
The Continent

Astera

The oldest known continent.

Not because it's the first...

Because it's the only one anyone remembers.

Everything beyond the sea is lost.

Ships never return.

Nobody knows why.

The Age

Thousands of years ago...

Magic wasn't learned.

Magic was alive.

Crystals grew naturally from the earth.

Forests breathed.

Mountains whispered.

Animals possessed intelligence.

People didn't cast spells.

They asked the world for permission.

Then...

Everything changed.

THE FRACTURE

Nobody agrees what happened.

Historians argue.

Priests disagree.

Old journals contradict each other.

Even the oldest spirits refuse to answer.

Only one thing is universally accepted.

The World Broke.

The sky cracked.

Entire civilizations disappeared overnight.

Magic became unstable.

Crystals began mutating.

Entire regions became corrupted.

Ancient cities vanished beneath forests.

Thousands of years later...

Nobody has rebuilt.

PRESENT DAY

Civilization survives.

Barely.

People stay close to settlements.

Nobody willingly enters corrupted lands.

Explorers rarely return.

The ones that do...

Come back changed.

THE PLAYER

The player is not chosen.

Not a hero.

Not royalty.

Not the prophecy.

They're simply...

Someone who refuses to stop asking questions.

That curiosity changes everything.

WHY THE PLAYER CAN USE MAGIC

Nobody knows.

Not even the player.

The first crystal reacts differently.

The Wizard notices immediately.

He says nothing.

THE MAIN MYSTERY

What caused the Fracture?

Every world should answer one question...

...while creating two new ones.

THE CRYSTALS

Crystals are alive.

Not sentient.

Alive.

They remember.

Old crystals hold memories.

Ancient crystals change nearby wildlife.

Some whisper.

Some sing.

Some cry.

Nobody knows why.

DANGER ZONES

They aren't monster nests.

They're scars.

Places where reality never fully healed.

Each danger zone tells part of history.

Not exposition.

Environment.

Architecture.

Creatures.

Music.

Weather.

Everything.

THE FACTIONS
The Arcane Guild

Scholars.

Researchers.

Explorers.

Believe knowledge fixes everything.

Crystal Wardens

Protect settlements.

Destroy corrupted crystals.

Distrust magic.

The Hollow

People who disappeared...

...and returned.

Nobody knows what happened to them.

They speak strangely.

Sometimes finish sentences...

...before you start them.

The Forgotten

No one knows who they are.

Only ruins remain.
